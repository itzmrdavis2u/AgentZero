# Alexander "Alex" Kane (Codename: ZERO)

**Role / Archetype:** Deniable Ghost Operative / Burned Special Forces Operator  
**Logline:** Officially killed in action during a cover-up he wasn't meant to survive, a former Special Forces operative becomes "Zero"—a man who doesn't exist, hunting the dark figures who act silently in the shadows of the British government.

---

## Core Psychology (Film & Narrative)
- **External Want:** Expose Blackstar, the secret group working within the government (the present mission, Operation Blackstar).
- **Internal Need:** Relearn how to trust without letting paranoia turn him into the very kind of cold manipulator he is hunting.
- **Fatal Flaw / Blind Spot:** Radical distrust. Because his trusted CO betrayed him after Operation Nightfall, Zero refuses to rely on allies—leaving himself isolated, predictable to enemies who know he works alone, and vulnerable to walking into traps set by his new mysterious controller.
- **Red Lines:** He has several. The main ones: he will not harm an innocent person, and absolutely never a child. *(Others to be defined: see `story-expansion-log.md`.)*
- **Signature Rule / Philosophy:** *"Everybody leaves a trace."* Zero’s core specialty is forensic observation, pattern recognition, and exploiting the tiny human mistakes others miss.

## Voice & Dialogue
- **Speech Cadence:** Measured, economical, and unhurried. Never raises his voice when things go wrong; the more dangerous the situation, the quieter and drier he becomes.
- **Verbal Tics / Vocabulary:** Understated British dry wit; operational shorthand; uses questions to test what the other person already knows rather than volunteering information.
- **Sample Line (Under Pressure):** *"If I'm dead, what exactly am I supposed to be?"*

## Visual & Physical Presence (Concept Art / 3D)
- **Silhouette & Build:** Early 40s, lean and functional rather than bulky—built for endurance, blending into crowds, and close-quarters efficiency.
- **Key Wardrobe / Props:** Understated tailored dark suits when operating in diplomatic/corporate spaces; matte, low-profile tactical gear without unit patches or serial numbers when things go kinetic. Carries a legacy encrypted comms unit that shouldn't exist.
- **Image Prompt Seed:** `British man in his early 40s, sharp observant eyes, weathered calm expression, dark tailored minimalist suit, understated luxury watch, moody cinematic lighting, realistic espionage thriller aesthetic, 35mm film grain`

## Backstory & Timeline
- **Military Origins:** Joined the British Army young; stood out not for raw aggression, but for preternatural calm under fire and tactical judgment (knowing *when not* to fight). Progressed into Tier-1 special operations overseas.
- **Operation Nightfall (The Beginning):** Before Agent Zero existed, Sergeant Alex Kane was a highly respected Special Forces operator. His team was sent into a hostile country after intelligence suggested a remote military complex was secretly producing weapons of mass destruction. Orders were recon only: infiltrate, confirm whether the programme exists, photograph anything useful, identify key personnel, get out undetected.
  - **The photograph:** Separated from his team, Kane took an alternative route and entered what appeared to be the private office of the man running the facility. He photographed documents and maps. Then he noticed a framed photograph of several men at a private function. He recognised two of them as senior members of the British Government, standing beside the very man he'd been sent to investigate. They looked like friends, not enemies at a diplomatic meeting. He photographed it.
  - **The first mistake:** Back in Britain, Kane reported what he found to his commanding officer, someone he trusted completely (perhaps a mentor who'd known him for years). The CO seemed concerned, studied the photograph, and said: *"Leave this with me. Don't mention it in your report. Not yet."* He said that if senior officials had links to the weapons programme, it had to be handled carefully, and that Kane did the right thing bringing it to him. Kane believed him. That was his first mistake.
  - **The cover-up:** The official report left out the room Kane found and the photograph. Its conclusion: no evidence of a weapons programme. Kane confronted the CO, who was now cold: *"You saw what you were sent there to see, Sergeant Kane. Nothing more."* When Kane mentioned the photograph, the CO said: *"What photograph?"* That's when Kane realised something was seriously wrong.
  - **Kane becomes a problem:** The CO had investigated the photograph, and in doing so alerted the people Kane had uncovered. A secret network exists inside the British establishment: politicians, intelligence officials, military officers, businessmen, defence contractors, and foreign intermediaries. They aren't necessarily trying to destroy Britain. They believe they're protecting it, and for years they've secretly manipulated conflicts, governments and arms deals to maintain Britain's influence. The weapons facility is part of something much larger. Kane doesn't know any of this yet. He only knows someone wants that photograph buried, and that they now know he's seen it.
  - **The hidden original (the twist):** Kane didn't tell his CO everything. Special Forces training taught him never to surrender the only copy of valuable intelligence. The image he showed the CO was downloaded from his equipment, and he secretly kept the original memory card. Later he looked at the photograph again and enlarged it. Something in the background he'd missed (a symbol on the wall, a partially obscured person, or a handwritten date; the exact detail is still to be defined) led him, by tracing it, to a name: **BLACKSTAR**. He first assumed Blackstar was the weapons programme. He was wrong: Blackstar is the organisation protecting it.
- **Operation Blackstar (The Present Mission):** **Blackstar** is the code name of the secret group of people working within the government. **Operation Blackstar** is the operation Agent Zero is currently on to expose them. Kane first learned the name from the photograph he kept after Nightfall.
- **The Mission That Kills Alex Kane (operation name to be defined):** Kane eventually receives orders for another operation, supposedly completely unrelated. His unit is sent abroad to capture a high-value target. He goes because refusing would reveal his suspicions. Once there he realises something is wrong: the intelligence is too perfect, the route leaves them exposed, their extraction is cancelled, and then communications disappear. He understands there is no mission. The operation was constructed to eliminate him. But whoever arranged it made one enormous mistake: they sent Special Forces to kill a Special Forces soldier. Kane survives (badly injured, leading to the year-long coma). Officially, Alexander Kane is killed in action. His family and his fiancée are informed, a funeral is held, and his military records are sealed. Somewhere inside Whitehall, someone receives a short encrypted message: **KANE RESOLVED.**
- **The Empty Coffin:** Kane was officially declared KIA with a sealed record and an empty casket. His family was led to believe he was dead, and the agency he worked for believed (or hoped) he was dead. After the coma and his recovery, the controller made contact, and his underground phase began then. He spent it learning who he was hunting.
- **The Controller's Secret (hidden from Zero for now):** The controller is the one who made sure Kane was taken to a hospital and survived. Zero does not know this at first. It becomes known much later in the story. *(Reveal to be expanded: see `story-expansion-log.md`.)*
- **Evelyn Cross & the Life He Was Leaving For:** Kane helped rescue Evelyn Cross on an earlier mission (details undisclosed). They fell for each other at once and were engaged about 2 months after meeting. She believed he was an Army sergeant whose job took him away on missions. About 3 months in she told him she was pregnant, one week before the mission that led to his faked death. Getting out of the spy game for a normal life with her was his goal.
- **Jenny:** Jenny was unborn when Kane "died", so he has never met her. He learned of her from his controller. He isn't sure he is her father, but he longs to be. She is about 7 now.
- **Timeline:** About 7 years have passed between his apparent death and the present story. Kane is in his early 40s now. He was a Special Forces operative at the time of his death, and he only became Agent Zero after he woke from his coma (to be expanded later).
- **The Coma & Recovery:** The injuries Kane sustained on the mission of his "death" left him in a coma for a year. After he woke, he recovered his memories over time. *(To be expanded: see `story-expansion-log.md`.)*
- **Becoming Zero:** Summoned via a dead comms channel with a photograph, coordinates, and a black dossier marked only with `0`. Recruited by an unnamed controller—leaving Kane perpetually questioning whether he's finally fighting the conspiracy or simply serving another layer of it.
